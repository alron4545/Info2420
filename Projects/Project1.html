Project 1 Questions

  1. The site really fits the theme of Trader Joe’s. The typeface as well as the graphics throughout the site fit the nautical and tropical theme. I like the crafted graphics it uses and love the side navigationbar populated with unique icons.
  2. The site is laid out almost like a newspaper, with horizontal lines separating the different types of content. Much of the content fills the entire screen space, making the information easier to read. Almost like reading different articles. In addition to this, there is a well designed sidebar that directs you to different pages within the website. For a better overview of all the subpages, there is menu section that can be brought up by clicking the three bars on the top left bar. This shows the subpages and categories of the 5 side menu items.
  3. This site structures makes sense to me as there isn’t a million menus and submenus. There is only 5 menu items and they are very clear where they will lead you to. You don’t need to guess where a menu is to access what you need. A search bar also makes it easy to access what you need.
  4. I think the design enhances the site and makes for a more user friendly experience. It matches the theme of trade joes with the black and white “sketched “ icons as well as the tropical pirate/trader newspaper feel. All the icons add to the theme and none seem to distract.
  5. The load time was very reasonable and the jumping between different pages felt snappy. The minimalism of animations and effects made it feel responsive, and I found no major problem areas where content needed to load for a long time. For context I accessed this on Chrome on an 2023 M3 Mac, running the Tahoe IOS - 120 MBS Download internet speed at time of testing. Any times content needed to load, a red loading circle appear, but only for maybe a 10th of a second at most. The slowest loading spots were selecting the my store page. As well as populating the grocery items, after pressing on a category such as Dairy. On average, these took about 1.5 seconds on average. The actual home page took roughly 1 second to load fully. These is a workable speed for me and for most of the general population.
  6. The text color was simple in its color scheme. Only black and red was used, making it easy to read. Red was used primarily as labels, while the actual content was black. The background color was as simple well, utilizing only white and a sepia like color. This fits the theme for the site, and puts the focus on the actual content on the site. Nothing is screaming for attendant, and it gives the calming feeling of reading a newspaper.
  7. There are 4 major navigation sections of the pages. The top bar which includes Careers and a Stores tabs. The main pop out navigation page accessed by clicking the three lines on the top left of the page. The side bar, which includes pages represented by icons such as Recipes, and Discover. Finally, at the bottom, there is an index of categorized different pages. I think it is well done this way as the complicated more populated indexes are located at the bottom, and behind the three bars. This gives the user a good first impression, and doesn’t overwhelm them. The simple side navigation bar is also a nice choice and it establishes a unique aseptic, and well as invites the user to actually use to this. It makes it feel exploratory rather than technical.
  8. The whole site uses white and sepia backgrounds, with black and red font colors. This is on brand for Trader Joe’s which goes for the tropical tiki like aseptic. The site doesn’t use the colorful, floral like aesthetic though. Rather, it goes for the more informational newspaper-like color scheme. It uses this theme across all the pages, giving it a cohesive theme.
  9. This site responds well with resizing its window. The content always fills the horizontal space it is given, without excess empty spots. As the horizontal space is narrowed, it first gets rid of the side navigation bar. This is followed by then only allowing one column of content. Even with one column, the content is still formatted well, and feels complete. In addition, instead of trying to cram label text for the navigation bar, it gets rid of the text and only uses the symbol. The narrow resized window makes it very similar to a mobile website. Vertical resizing does not change the formatting and only hides the content.
  10. The big highlight of this website was the Icon based side navigation bar, as well as the graphics that were used. These graphics feel very Trader Joes which gives it a unique feel. You feel as if a human hand crafted this rather than coded. Another highlight is the responsiveness of the site. Many other grocery sites are very sluggish and always feel cluttered. This is fast and minimal, and overall well designed (at least compared to Kroger or Albertsons).
